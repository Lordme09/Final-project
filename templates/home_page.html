Reason

People often find themselves with items they no longer need or looking for a new one. CommuintyGive helps connect people so that items can find themselves in someone's hand who might need them instead of ending up in the landfill.

Mission

CommunityGive's goal is to make donating easy and encourage community support. Along with that, the hope is to reduce unnecessary waste and help people find items they might need.
